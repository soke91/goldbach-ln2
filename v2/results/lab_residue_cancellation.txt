  DIAGNOSTIC (post hoc). V4's instrument is the wrong one and
  its correlations say so: -0.90, -0.82, -0.76, -0.56, +0.98,
  wandering in sign. The gain l1/|R| is a ratio whose
  DENOMINATOR passes near zero -- a k at which R happens to
  cancel almost completely contributes an enormous gain -- so
  the octave mean of the ratio is set by outliers. The
  aggregate gain, mean(l1) over mean(|R|), has no such
  denominator problem and is what V4 meant to ask:
