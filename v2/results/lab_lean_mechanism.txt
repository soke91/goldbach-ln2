  DIAGNOSTIC (post hoc). What the profile actually is.
  The premise of the Mertens reading is only half true --
  M(x) is negative at most small x but not at all of them:
  range of x   fraction with M(x) < 0
  [2    ,30    ] 0.9655
  [2    ,100   ] 0.8687
  [2    ,1000  ] 0.6547
  [2    ,10000 ] 0.5630
  and P3 shows the correspondence is absent anyway: the
  agreement sits near the permutation median, and the best
  draw beats it at every N. The lean is not the Mertens
  function seen through a short window.

  What the octave table shows instead is a definite profile in
  N/k with a POSITIVE lean at the shortest sums, a minimum in
  the middle, and a slow return toward 1/2:
  N            argmin octave   f+ there   f+ at [2,4)
  200000       [16    ,32     ) 0.0769     0.7368
  400000       [16    ,32     ) 0.0770     0.7355
  800000       [16    ,32     ) 0.0830     0.7366
  1600000      [16    ,32     ) 0.0887     0.7347
  3200000      [16    ,32     ) 0.0936     0.7325
  The positive lean at N/k < 4 has an elementary cause: N is
  even, so N - mk is even whenever mk is, and Lambda vanishes
  on even numbers above 2. For odd k only odd m survive, so
  the m = 1 term stands almost alone there and it is
  nonnegative. That is parity, not cancellation, and it says
  the short-sum end of the profile carries no information
  about the wall.
