  DIAGNOSTIC (post hoc). Why a widening window inflates the
  growth. c_R is an average of |R|/sqrt(N/k) over k, and if
  that ratio is not flat in k the average depends on where it
  stops. At the top rung, by octave of N/k:
  N/k octave        mean |R|/sqrt(N/k)   #k
  128               2.4543               7537
  512               2.4909               8491
  2048              2.6116               2128
  8192              2.5484               534
  32768             2.5052               128
  131072            2.0397               35
  524288            3.2515               10
  A window that grows with N samples a different part of
  this profile at every rung -- but K2 refutes that as the
  explanation: the FIXED window gives a STEEPER growth, not a
  shallower one. The profile is not flat, and that is the
  point: |R|/sqrt(N/k) falls with N/k, so c_R has no value
  independent of where the average stops.
