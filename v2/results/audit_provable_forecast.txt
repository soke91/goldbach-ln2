  and what the two answers are answers TO, which is not the
  same question. A is a measured maximum ratio, not a
  constant any theorem supplies: freezing it at the largest
  observed value is the conservative choice for an UPPER
  BOUND on the spend, and letting it fall is a description
  of what the data do, which would eventually put the
  'bound' below |P| itself and stop being a bound at all.
  So 5474.8 is where the bound provably pays and 9.0 to 10.3
  is where the measured ratio would; the two differ by
  5465 decades and neither corrects the other.

  what that leaves. The published 5474.8 is a forecast about
  a frozen constant, and the constant is not one: it falls
  at 10.30 standard errors and its shape is tied at 0.44 of
  the r.m.s.'s own error. Nothing here corrects the figure,
  because correcting it would need A's shape over four
  thousand decades -- an extrapolation this repository
  refuses on its own standard, {#rem:forecastbracket}.
