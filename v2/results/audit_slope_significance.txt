  DIAGNOSTIC on M1 (post hoc). The tolerance was set at 1e-5
  without asking what the printed tables can carry. A
  least-squares slope is a fixed linear functional of the
  ordinates, slope = sum c_i y_i with
  c_i = (x_i - xbar) / sum (x_j - xbar)^2, so rounding the
  y column to d decimals moves the refit by at most
  sum |c_i| * 0.5 * 10^-d. That bound is computable and is
  not a free parameter:
  series                       M1 diff      rounding bound covered?
  family level exponent        0.0000321    0.0000433      yes
  ladder level exponent        0.0000020    0.0000197      yes
  ladder k-exponent            0.0000016    0.0000197      yes
  ladder log rho               0.0000053    0.0000185      yes
  every M1 gap inside its own rounding bound: yes
  So M1 fails as written and the reason is arithmetic I
  imposed, not a disagreement with the published fits. The
  rule stands as registered; what it refutes is the
  tolerance, and no published slope is thereby in doubt.
