audit_cn_law.py's P2 and the sd half of its P4 are artefacts of a
single sign pattern. V is the second moment in expectation over
signs and no one pattern -- mu included -- has mean square exactly V
over a band. Both stay REFUTED as registered; what they refute is
the reading, not prop:V and not mu.
The kurtosis is different. No coin draw comes near the real arm's
value, so G's tails are not what a sign pattern on mu's support
produces. **That is one statistic of the withdrawn law that the
coin cannot make** -- the first thing in this branch to survive
lem:coin.
