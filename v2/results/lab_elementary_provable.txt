  dL does not drift at all: every N in this sweep has the same
  odd radical, so the admissible k-set and therefore dL are
  identical across it. That is a fact about the sweep, not a
  general one, and a sweep over different radicals would have
  to remeasure it.

  A is a different animal and is NOT declared as a drift. It
  falls monotonically with N because the maximum of the ratio
  is always attained at the shortest inner sum available,
  N over the k-cap, and that grows with N. It is a function of
  where one looks, not a wobbling constant, and the forecast
