why the table does not rise -- diagnosed after the run, predicted by nothing
  the weights are (dm/se)^2 and dm/se itself decays: {#rem:maskdmse} measured
  the amplitude exponent at 0.6289 and the error's at 0.0420, so the weight falls
  like N to the minus twice their difference. Bands added past the observed
  range carry almost no weight, and the effective sample size above stops
  growing while the band count doubles. **The design cannot answer the question
  it was registered to ask**, and the refutation of H3 and H4 is that, not a
  fact about the two forms. What is real underneath it is that the informative
  window is bounded: the mask sinks below its own error, and range past that
  point adds bands that measure nothing.
