  DIAGNOSTIC (post hoc). Where V1 and V4 broke, and by how much.
  The two N at which the model missed are the two at which the
  calibration moved most between the two ranges. Since
  B ~ c K^{1/2} log K, an error in c must be amplified into the
  crossing by roughly 1/(1/2 + 1/log K). Where the drift is
  below one per cent the ratio is dominated by the discreteness
  of the k-grid and means nothing; read only the rows with a
