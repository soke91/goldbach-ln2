  DIAGNOSTIC (post hoc). Why U2's band was wrong. The naive
  sampling error of a correlation over n points is 1/sqrt(n),
  which at these n would be about 0.0099 -- but the inner sums
  are NESTED: consecutive k share almost all their m, so the
  effective degrees of freedom are the number of octaves, not
  the number of k. The coin's own spread is the empirical band,
