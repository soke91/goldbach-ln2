    1 costs 34% in RMS against M2 free, whose intercept is
    a = 1.03947 -- above 1, and WALKING DOWN: 1.174,

    the data have not arrived at 1, and fixing it there is
    premature at this size rather than wrong. Proposition W
    predicts the limit, not how fast a finite window
    reaches it.

    over the same bands and is still going. So the 1/log N
    rate is supported and the limit is not yet reached by
    the data; rho -> 1 remains an inference from Chowla.
