    !! READ THIS BEFORE READING THE NUMBERS !!
    This relation is NEARLY ALGEBRAIC, not an independent
    test. beta is the slope of log sd(C) on log N; alpha is
    the slope of log sd(Z)^2 on log log N; and sd(C) is
    sd(Z) times a factor ~sqrt(S*N). So the two are the same
    numbers in two parametrisations, and beta = 0.5 +
    (alpha/2)(D loglog/D log) holds to the extent that both
    regressions are linear. An agreement to 1e-3 below is
