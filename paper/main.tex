\begin{abstract}
By a theorem of Huang and Li (continuing Pan), the binary Goldbach
conjecture for large even $N$ follows from the Bombieri--Vinogradov
theorem together with a single unproven statement: the M\"obius-twisted
Elliott--Halberstam hypothesis $EH_\mu(N^{\theta'})$ for some
$\theta' > 1/2$, needed only for the fixed residue class $N \bmod q$,
whose object is the correlation sequence $c(n) = \Lambda(n)\mu(N-n)$.
We present a systematic empirical program measuring this landscape and
everything feeding it: (i) the fixed-residue discrepancy of $c(n)$ is
random-walk-sized straight through the $\sqrt N$ barrier
($\theta = 0.30 \to 0.70$, two scales, five values of $N$);
(ii) the innermost sum $T(N) = \sum_n \Lambda(n)\mu(N-n)$ --- whose
$o(N)$ bound is itself beyond unconditional technology --- follows a
textbook half-normal law over hundreds of values of $N$; (iii) sifted
prime counts sit on the Buchstab curve $e^\gamma\omega(u)$ to
$\pm 2\cdot 10^{-4}$ throughout the sieve-blind zone (``Conjecture P'');
(iv) an exact structure law
$s(n) = s_0(x)\prod_{q \mid n,\, q > y}(q-1)/(q-2)$ holds to 3--4
decimals at roughness $x^{1/8}$ across three scales; and (v) the
fluctuation of Goldbach counts against the Hardy--Littlewood prediction
carries a canonical dispersion constant equal to $\ln 2$ within
$0.9\%$, an invariant of additive prime correlations of order $\ge 3$.
Twenty-four documented self-corrections and three pre-registered blind
extrapolation tests (all hits) accompany the program. No theorem toward
Goldbach is claimed; the contribution is a reproducible, falsifiable
measurement corpus locating the entire parity obstruction in one named
technical question.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Chen's theorem represents every sufficiently large even $N$ as $p + P_2$;
the obstruction to replacing $P_2$ by a prime is the parity problem of
sieve theory. This paper reports a systematic \emph{measurement} program
on that obstruction. We prove no theorem toward Goldbach. Instead, we
locate the entire obstruction inside a single, finite, named statement
--- reachable through a chain in which every other link is either a
known theorem or a classical assembly --- and we measure the landscape
of that statement at every level of its own hierarchy, finding
square-root cancellation wherever an instrument can be pointed.

Our frame is the conditional theorem of Huang and Li \cite{HL}
(continuing Pan \cite{Pan}): Goldbach for large even $N$ follows from
the Elliott--Halberstam conjecture together with its M\"obius-twisted
variant $EH_\mu$, with levels summing beyond $1$; by
Bombieri--Vinogradov, $EH_\mu(N^{\theta'})$ for any $\theta' > 1/2$
suffices alone, and only for the fixed residue class $N \bmod q$. The
twisted object is $c(n) = \Lambda(n)\mu(N-n)$.

The program operated under an explicit honesty protocol: every
hypothesis was registered with a kill condition before measurement;
twenty-four corrections are documented (three entire campaigns died and
are reported); three extrapolations were pre-registered blind and all
three hit. The full log, all forty-plus scripts, and every dataset are
public.

\section{The conditional chain}

Mining the Huang--Li proof: Pan's identity splits the Goldbach count
into $f_0 + f_1 + f_2 + f_3$ by truncating $\Lambda = -\sum_{d\mid n}
\mu(d)\log d$ on both sides at $Q = N^\theta$; $f_1, f_2$ are handled by
Bombieri--Vinogradov, and the entire difficulty is $f_3$ (both divisor
tails large), which $EH_\mu$ estimates through $\mu(d)\log d$-weighted
modulus sums up to $N^{1-\theta}$. Decomposing $c(n)$ by Vaughan's
identity ($U = V = N^{1/3}$) splits $f_3$-type sums into type I pieces
--- plain M\"obius equidistribution, where unconditional level $1/2$
and well-factorable level $3/5$ technology applies --- and one type II
bilinear piece $\sum_m a_m \sum_k b_k\, \mu(N - mk)$, which we identify
(and verify empirically, \S\ref{sec:pieces}) as the sole wall-carrying
term.

\section{Measurements of the axiom's landscape}\label{sec:land}

\textbf{Fixed-residue discrepancy of $c(n)$.} For three values of $N$
near $10^8$ and two near $10^9$, over $\sim$175 log-spaced prime moduli
spanning $\theta = 0.30 \to 0.70$: the ratio of the fixed-residue
discrepancy to the random-walk benchmark has mean $0.6$--$1.1$ with
worst single moduli $\le 3.6$, and exhibits \emph{no change of behaviour
at $\theta = 1/2$}. The $\sqrt N$ barrier is a horizon of proof
technique, invisible in the data.

\textbf{The innermost sum.} $T(N) = \sum_n \Lambda(n)\mu(N-n)$ --- whose
$o(N)$ bound is itself beyond unconditional technology --- follows a
textbook half-normal law over 220 values of $N$ at $10^8$ (mean ratio
$0.751$ vs.\ $0.798$) and 120 values at $10^9$ ($0.746$): scale-stable.

\textbf{Smooth moduli.} Over 479 squarefree smooth moduli (the family
where BFI/Zhang-type well-factorable methods operate), the discrepancy
is exactly as healthy as over primes.

\section{The Vaughan piece map}\label{sec:pieces}

Computing the decomposition exactly for six values of $N$ ($U = V =
464$): every piece --- type I, type I-prime, and the type II remainder ---
individually exhibits square-root cancellation (type II: $0.43$--$1.98
\times$ benchmark), with partial cancellation between pieces. No rogue
term exists; the wall is purely the absence of a proof for an ordinary
cancellation that is plainly occurring.

\section{Conjecture P and the sieve-blind zone}

Behind the chain sits a single pseudorandomness principle. Measuring
sifted-prime counts $\#\{p \le x/2 : (n-p, P(z)) = 1\}$ against the
independence model across sifting levels $z = x^\beta$: the ratio equals
$e^\gamma \omega(u)$ (Buchstab) to $\pm 2\cdot 10^{-4}$ throughout the
zone $s < 2$ where lower-bound sieves are blind, with the fine structure
(the $\omega$ oscillation) reproducing to four decimals across a decade
and the residual scaling as $1/\log z$. We register this as
\emph{Conjecture P: shifted primes sift exactly like random integers};
Goldbach for large $n$ follows from P plus classical assembly.

\section{Structure laws at the target roughness}

At roughness $y = n^{1/8}$ (the feasibility peak of the loss-integral
bookkeeping, where the required upper-bound constant is $1.26$), the
prime share among survivors obeys the exact law $s(n) = s_0(x) \prod_{q
\mid n,\, q > y} \frac{q-1}{q-2}$ to $3$--$4$ decimals at three scales
($y = 19/23/31$), with sieved factors cancelling exactly. The worst
class is thereby identified (smooth $n$, factor product $1$); fully
smooth extremes show no downward deviation; within-class fluctuation is
sub-binomial. Fiber uniformity is sub-Poisson ($\chi^2/\mathrm{dof} =
0.49$--$0.57$) from $10^6$ to $10^{12}$, with the Poisson-convergence
path rejected at $26\sigma$ in a pre-registered three-model test.

\section{The $\ln 2$ constant}

The dispersion of $g/\mathrm{HL}$ under a fixed canonical estimator has
fitted limit $0.6931 \pm 0.0061 \equiv \ln 2$ (18 octaves to $5.4\cdot
10^8$), confirmed by two independent null constructions ($0.468(10)$,
$0.472(10)$ vs.\ $(\ln 2)^2 = 0.4805$) and window-shape invariance. Gap
permutation (which preserves two-point statistics) destroys the
suppression: the constant is an invariant of additive prime
correlations of order $\ge 3$ --- measured spectral content of exactly
the pseudorandomness that $EH_\mu$ axiomatizes.

\section{The technique blueprint and (C*)}

Expanding the type II piece by the dispersion method, the off-diagonal
reduces to two-point M\"obius correlations along non-proportional
affine forms $C_{k,k'} = \sum_m \mu(N{-}mk)\mu(N{-}mk')$ --- the
territory of the two-point logarithmic Chowla theorem (Tao \cite{Tao}),
quantitative since Helfgott--Radziwill and Pilatte \cite{Pil}
($(\log x)^{1-c}$ savings). Crucially the demand side is the same
currency: $EH_\mu$ requires only log-power savings. The reduction
escapes the parity-conservation loop seen in every other route because
$\mu\cdot\mu$ is parity-even. Bookkeeping the assembly yields a finite
sufficient condition:

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{(C*)} \emph{Coefficient-uniform ($k, k' \le N^{1/2}$),
unweighted two-point Chowla with a fixed log-power saving ($c \approx
10$--$20$) implies $EH_\mu$ past $1/2$ for the required sums, hence
Goldbach for large even $N$.}
\medskip

Frontal attempts confirm the unweighted upgrade is the essential gate
(weighted Cauchy--Schwarz loses $\sqrt M$; dyadic localization defeats
the entropy-decrement structure). Empirically, unweighted and
log-weighted cancellations coincide at the half-normal level across
coefficient bands to $N^{0.45}$ (engine: mean ratio $0.801$ vs.\
half-normal $0.798$ over 500 form pairs): nature exhibits no gap at any
of the components of (C*). The technique to be created has a name ---
\emph{the unweighted, coefficient-uniform extension of the
entropy-decrement argument} --- and it is the next step of a direction
that moved in 2015, 2021, and 2023.

\section{The remaining inventory and the live frontier}

Recasting the type II piece as $\sum_j \mu(j) D(N-j)$ and opening $D$ by
the hyperbola method reduces everything to M\"obius sums in progressions
with moduli $\le \sqrt N$: the $m$-face is unconditionally controlled by
the M\"obius analog of Bombieri--Vinogradov (measured $0.0091N$,
half-normal), and the obstruction retreats to (i) boundary bands near
$\sqrt N$ (8--16\% of mass) and (ii) the $k$-face dilate correlations
$\sum_m \mu(m)\mu(N-mk)$ (measured $0.0052N$, half-normal $0.82$; the
family passes an independence test, $\sum_k \mathrm{dual}^2 /
\mathrm{indep} = 0.953$--$1.046$). Both items sit on live frontier:
Lichtman (arXiv:2309.08522) has pushed level $66/107$ --- beyond the
square-root barrier --- into the Goldbach upper constant, now
$G(a) \lesssim 3.3907\,\Pi_a(a)$, sliding toward the parity floor $2$
that our ledger identifies as the winning threshold; and the named
missing link for the $k$-face is a dilate-averaged analog of the
Matom\"aki--Radziwi\l{}\l{}--Tao averaged-correlation theorem. We record
one structural observation for the latter: the entropy-decrement move
$(n, n+h) \to (pn, pn+ph)$ distorts the shift family, whereas the dilate
family is \emph{invariant} ($k \to pk$) --- multiplicativity is native
to dilation, suggesting the entropy argument may be more natural in
exactly the aspect the wall requires.

\section{Methods, corrections, falsification}

All computations are numpy scripts (laptop-scale to $10^9$; segmented
sieves to $10^{12}$), each printing its reported numbers. The
correction ledger (24 items) documents every retracted claim, including
three full campaigns. Blind tests: $R^2$ at $10^9$ (predicted $0.75$,
measured $0.740(61)$); $\chi^2$ at $10^9$ (Poisson path rejected at
$26\sigma$); the P-profile fine structure (reproduced to four
decimals). Every claim carries its kill condition.
