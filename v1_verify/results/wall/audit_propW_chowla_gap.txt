    Consequence, stated exactly: feeding |S(h)| <= eps into
    the displayed formula gives |rho-1| <= eps * Gamma. At
    N = 4000000, Gamma = 3.580e+05, so a
    hypothesis of the form S(h) = o(1) leaves the bound
    |rho-1| = o(3.6e+05) -- vacuous, since
    rho >= 0 and the trivial bound on |rho-1| is already
    C(N)^2/V + 1. The strength actually required is
    S(h) = o(log N / N), not S(h) = o(1).
